Glossary

The terms used across these docs and in the app, in one place.

Actual cost
Labor cost plus expenses recorded so far. On a time-based project, actual hours.
Billing rate
What a client is charged per hour for a person's work. Separate from the cost rate, and used only when hourly revenue is on.
Budget
An optional target for a project: an amount in its currency, or hours on a time-based project. On a fixed-price project it is also the revenue.
Cost account
A named bucket of work (for example CAPEX, OPEX or a client) defined by JQL. It can carry its own rates and receive expenses. Managed site-wide by Jira administrators.
Cost project
The app's own entity: a scope plus everything that hangs off it (rates, team, expenses, revenue, budget). Not a copy of a Jira project.
Cost rate
What a person costs the business per hour.
Default hourly rate
The project's fallback rate, used when no own, account or role rate applies. A new project starts at 0.
Effective from
The date a rate starts to apply. Hours logged on or after it use the new rate; earlier hours keep their old one.
Expense
A cost that is not logged time: a licence, travel, a contractor invoice. One-off or recurring.
Fixed price
A revenue mode where the project's budget is its revenue regardless of hours logged.
JQL
Jira Query Language. Used to define a cost project's scope and a cost account's issues.
Labor cost
The sum of every worklog's hours times its rate.
Margin
Profit as a share of revenue.
Milestone
A date and a cumulative planned spend. Together they form the plan the Overview compares actual cost with.
Monetary-based
A project type whose cost and budget are in a currency, computed from hourly rates.
Period (of a rate)
A stretch of time during which a rate has one value. A rate with several periods has a history.
Portfolio
A group of cost projects with a combined view of cost, budget and team.
Profit
Revenue minus actual cost.
Role
A named group of people in one project with a shared cost and billing rate.
Scope
What a cost project reads: one or more Jira spaces, or a JQL filter. It resolves to one JQL string.
Space (Jira space)
Jira's name for a project container. "Jira space" and "Jira project" mean the same thing in these docs, to tell them apart from a cost project.
Time-based
A project type whose cost and budget are in hours. It uses no rates and has no revenue.
Worklog
A record of time logged on a Jira issue. Cost Tracker reads these; nobody logs time in the app.
