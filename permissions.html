Permissions and access

Who can do what in the app, and which Jira permissions the app itself asks for.

Who can do what#

Access is decided per project. See Sharing and ownership for how people are given a level.

ReportsA report's data is limited by the project it reads: you only see figures for projects you can open.

Hourly rates are tied to named people and can reveal compensation-related information. Who sees a project's rates is controlled by that project's sharing settings, and nothing in the app shows one project's rates to someone without access to it.

Permissions the app asks for#

The app requests three Forge permissions and nothing else:

PermissionUsed for
read:jira-workSearching issues and reading their worklogs and dates, listing Jira projects, and checking whether you are a site administrator.
read:jira-userShowing people's names and finding users to add as members or to share with.
storage:appKeeping the app's own data (projects, rates, expenses) in Forge storage.

Jira is only ever read, never written to. Requests to Jira run as the person using the app. Cost Tracker declares no external network access and no web triggers. Details: Privacy policy and Security policy.
