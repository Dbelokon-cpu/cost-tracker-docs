Portfolios

Group several cost projects for a combined view of cost, budget, team and timeline.

A portfolio groups cost projects so you can see their combined cost, budget and schedule. Open Portfolios in the sidebar to list them; the cards open on a click anywhere.

Create and fill a portfolio#

  1. Choose Create portfolio, name it and, optionally, describe it.
  2. Open it, go to the Summary tab and use Add projects. A searchable table with checkboxes lets you add or remove several at once.

Only projects you can see can be added.

The three tabs#

TabWhat it shows
SummaryCombined figures (total cost, total budget, team size, project count), the member projects with their own status, dates and revenue, and Export to XLSX.
TimelineEach project from its start to its end date as one bar, coloured by status, with a line for today. If a project has no dates, they are taken from its issues.
ConfigurationName, description, owner and projects, plus the portfolio's sharing.

The Summary shows its totals once every member has been calculated. Each member's figures arrive separately, so a slow project never blocks the page.

Mixed currencies and time-based projects#

Hours always add up. Money adds up only across monetary projects that share one currency. With mixed currencies, or only time-based members, the money totals are left out and the page says so, rather than adding unlike units. Amounts are not converted between currencies.

Deleted and capped members#

  • If a member project is deleted, the portfolio still loads and notes that the member was excluded from the totals.
  • If a member hit the 500-issue limit, a warning names that project, so you know which total is incomplete.

Access#

A portfolio is private to its owner by default. The owner can share it with named people as read-only viewers. There is no "everyone" option and no editor level. Only the owner can delete the portfolio, add or remove projects and edit its description. See Sharing and ownership.
