A prepaid hours package (also called a retainer or a block of hours) is the time a customer has paid for in advance. Cost Tracker attaches the package to a cost account and draws it down with the time logged on that account's issues, so you can see at any moment how much is left and warn the customer before it runs out.

Set up a package

  1. Open Setup → Accounts and create or edit an account. Its JQL decides which issues count against the package, for example project = SUP AND labels = acme.
  2. In the Prepaid hours section, enter the Hours and choose how it Renews: Every month or One block of hours.
  3. Pick the Starts date and, optionally, the Ends date.
  4. Set Warn at, the share of the hours after which the package is marked as running low (80% by default).
  5. For a monthly package, tick Carry unused hours (and overruns) into the next month if your contract rolls hours over.
  6. Save. To remove the package later, clear the Hours field.
FieldNotes
HoursA number above 0. Empty means the account has no package.
RenewsEvery month: the hours are given again on each calendar month, the first period starting on the start date. One block of hours: one amount for the whole contract.
Starts, EndsStarts is required. Work logged before it does not count. With no end date, a monthly package keeps renewing.
Warn atA whole percent from 1 to 100.
Carry overMonthly packages only. Unused hours are added to the next month, and an overrun is taken from it.

See the balance

The Prepaid hours tab under Setup → Accounts lists every account with a package: its customer, the package, a bar for the current period, the hours left, a status and the date the hours are expected to run out at the current pace. A note at the top lists the packages that need attention, and a filter narrows the list to them.

A package's page

Click an account to open its package:

  • Included, Used, Left, Runs out for the current period. Included shows the hours carried in from the previous month, if any.
  • By month: every period with its hours, carry-over, use and balance. Click a month to see its work.
  • Issues in the month: each issue worked on in that period with its hours, largest first.
  • Export to XLSX saves the chosen month (the package, the balance and the issue list) as a file you can send to the customer.

How hours are counted

  • Every worklog on an issue matching the account's JQL counts, anywhere on the site. Unlike cost, the package is not limited to one cost project's scope, because a customer's package usually covers all their work.
  • A worklog counts in the period of the day it was logged for.
  • The figures are counted from the Jira worklogs you can see. Someone without access to some issues sees a smaller use.
  • Results are kept for about 15 minutes. Refresh recounts at once. Changing any account also recounts.
  • The issue overlap rule for rates (an issue in several accounts goes to the first one) does not apply here: an issue counts against every package whose JQL it matches.
  • Up to 500 issues are read per package, from the earliest period shown. If more match, the page says the figures are incomplete; narrow the account's JQL.
  • Long monthly packages show the last 36 months.

Who can do what

Only Jira site administrators can set up or change a package, as with any account. Everyone can see the Prepaid hours tab and export a month.
