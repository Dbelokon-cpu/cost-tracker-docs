Who can open the app

By default everyone on the Jira site can open Cost Tracker. A Jira site administrator can limit it to chosen Jira groups under Setup → Access: then only members of those groups can open the app, and everyone else sees a message asking them to contact an administrator. Jira site administrators can always open it, so a wrong choice never locks everyone out. Clear the list to open the app to everyone again.

The limit covers the whole app, because costs, rates and revenue are what the app shows: hiding only the rates would still let them be worked out from costs and hours. A change takes effect within about a minute.

Hourly rates are tied to named people and can reveal compensation-related information. A project's own rates (people, roles, the default rate) are seen only by people who can open that project. Cost account and role library rates are site-wide and seen by everyone who can open the app, so if they are sensitive, limit the app to the right groups under Setup → Access.

Permissions the app asks for

The app requests four Forge permissions and nothing else:

PermissionUsed for
read:jira-workSearching issues and reading their worklogs and dates, listing Jira projects, and checking whether you are a site administrator.
read:jira-userShowing people's names, finding users to add as members or to share with, and listing Jira groups and checking your group membership for Setup → Access.
storage:appKeeping the app's own data (projects, rates, expenses) in Forge storage.
report:personal-dataAtlassian's weekly personal data check: the app tells Atlassian which account IDs it holds, and Atlassian answers which accounts were closed or changed. See Privacy policy.

Jira is only ever read, never written to. Requests to Jira run as the person using the app. Cost Tracker declares no external network access and no web triggers. Details: Privacy policy and Security policy.
