Budget

A budget is optional. Set it in Settings → General → Schedule and budget. On a monetary project the budget is an amount in the project's currency; on a time-based project it is a number of hours. With no budget, remaining budget and progress show a dash instead of a misleading figure.

On a fixed-price project the budget is also the project's revenue. See Revenue and profit.

Milestones: a plan of spend over time

Under the budget, Set budget milestones lets you plan how much should have been spent by given dates. Each milestone is a date and a cumulative amount, so every amount is the running total:

  • amounts may not fall as dates advance,
  • no amount may exceed the budget,
  • two milestones cannot share a date, and there can be at most 24.

On a time-based project the milestones are hours.

Example: Website redesign

Budget 6,000. Milestones: 1 Jun 1,000, 1 Jul 3,000, 1 Aug 5,000. If actual cost on 1 Jul is 3,400, the Overview reads "Plan to date: 3,000, spent 3,400 — 400 ahead of plan", in red.

Reading the Overview

The Overview shows schedule, scope and cost progress side by side, so an overrun appears against the same timeline that causes it, not just as one red number.

Progress barMeasures
ScheduleHow far through the project's timeframe today is.
ScopeIssues in a done status category out of all issues scanned, with the number remaining.
Cost (or Hours)Actual cost against the budget, with the amount remaining or the amount over budget.

Two lines can appear under the bars:

  • Plan to date (when milestones exist): what the plan says should have been spent by now, what was spent, and the difference, shown as "ahead of plan" (spent more than planned), "under plan" or "exactly on plan".
  • Forecast by the end date (when recurring expenses exist): actual cost plus the recurring expenses still to come, and by how much that would exceed the budget.

The monthly cost chart shows labor and expenses per month, and an Accounts card splits cost by account category such as CAPEX and OPEX. If the scope was capped at the 500-issue limit, the Overview says so under the figures.
